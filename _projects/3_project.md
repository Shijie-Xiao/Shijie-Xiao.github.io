Graduate research at the [Climate Risk & Extreme Dynamics Lab](https://sarhadi.eas.gatech.edu/), Georgia Tech, advised by Prof. Ali Sarhadi.

## Overview

_Physics-based Generative AI for Probabilistic Forecasting of Hurricane-Driven Compound Flooding_ (in preparation for NMI)

This project builds a generative emulator that translates tropical cyclone tracks into compound flood fields for probabilistic hazard forecasting.

## Key Components

- **Generative emulator**: a TensorFlow-based model translating tropical cyclone tracks into compound flood fields
- **Synthetic training data**: massive ensembles of physics-simulated synthetic tracks coupled with complex hydrodynamic and hydraulic simulations, overcoming historical data scarcity
- **Real-time probabilistic maps**: flood maps generated from ECMWF ensembles in seconds instead of days, enabling risk-informed emergency planning
