Undergraduate research at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Xiang Bai.

## Overview

_Recognition and Extraction of Musical Notes Using Optical Character Recognition_

This work applies optical character recognition techniques to text detection of musical notation.

## Key Components

- **Dataset construction**: built and annotated a specialized dataset from online musical tune data for training and evaluation
- **Model evaluation**: benchmarked DBNet, DBNet++, and PANet using the MMOCR framework and detection libraries
