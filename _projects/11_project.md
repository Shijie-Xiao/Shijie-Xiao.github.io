Undergraduate project (June 2020 - May 2021).

## Overview

A health management system with QR health codes and trip cards, supporting registration, trip tracking, and epidemic risk management.

## Key Components

- **QR health code**: generation and dynamic color coding of health status
- **Data management**: a structured user database supporting registration, trip tracking, and automated status updates
- **Risk evaluation**: location-based epidemic risk level updates
