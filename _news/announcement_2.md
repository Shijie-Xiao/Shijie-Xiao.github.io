Defended my master's thesis, _A Computational Framework for Multi-Agent Behavior Analysis: Integrating Geometric Vision Extraction and Social Attention Inference_, at the BrainML Lab, Georgia Tech.

---

The thesis presents a computational framework for analyzing multi-animal interactions and social behavior, combining geometric vision extraction (pose and trajectory estimation from video) with social attention inference that models directed interactions between agents.

The project page is available [here](https://Shijie-Xiao.github.io/projects/1_project/).
